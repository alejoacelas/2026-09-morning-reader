# Morning reader

An Android app that replaces a morning Twitter/Feedly habit with a bounded
session of reading blocks: self-contained 5–15 minute passages from books and
blog posts longer than 5 minutes. It explores whether a finite, curated feed can
keep what makes feeds appealing (variety, low commitment) without the endless
scroll.

The phone is a Moto G35 5G (Android 15). Connect with `~/best/fun/adb-phone`.

Secrets live in `.env` (ignored). Rebuild it from 1Password as the README describes.
